Accord Project (agreement layer for agentic commerce)

Linux Foundationspecificationcontext entry

Verified 2026-10-16. Status as of 2026-10-15. All entries.

Status

Open-source smart legal contract stack of Accord Project, a Series of LF Projects, LLC; its documentation page "Agentic Commerce" (no date on the page, last changed 2026-07-07 in the repository history, read 2026-10-15) describes it as the agreement layer beneath authorization and checkout protocols. The Concerto model of x402 presented on 2026-07-22 is pull request #194 of accordproject/models, open on 2026-10-15 (last update 2026-08-05).

Relevance

The documentation page says "Accord Project is the agreement layer" and calls its three integration patterns (an authorization credential binding a contract by hash, Concerto as schema language for a constraint registry, contract logic for terms "authorization can't enforce") "illustrative", describing "technical fit, not partnerships or endorsements" with AP2, UCP, ACP and Verifiable Intent.

Two prototypes by individual members of the project's Technical Steering Committee (TSC), outside the project's repositories and without licence or release, apply them with stubbed or simulated settlement: mttrbrts/agentic-obligations (last commit 2026-05-20) and The-Building-Blocks/accord-x402-contract-server (last commit 2026-07-30).

They bind a contract reference in AP2 mandates and in x402 payment requirements, and the second says the x402 payment signature "does not sign the Accord agreementHash".

Committed next

  • Q3 2026 review, "Plans & Upcoming Work": "Publish the agentic commerce blog series, starting with x402 and followed by A2A, with a docs page for each protocol". The review's summary of the meeting of 2026-07-22 records an agreement: "The group agreed to take x402 all the way through model, example, docs and blog post before releasing the AP2, A2A and other bindings." (undated) [maintainer or chair statement] https://accordproject.org/news/accord-project-tech-working-group-q3-2026-in-review/ (opens in a new tab)

Boundary

The documentation page: "Accord Project is not an identity, authorization, or payment layer. It does not issue credentials, perform key binding, or implement selective disclosure"; its footer says "The Accord Project is not a standards setting body or organization."
