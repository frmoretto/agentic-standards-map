schema.org open issues relevant to agents, bookings, product and establishment identity

Independentwork-itemcore entry

Verified 2026-10-14. Status as of 2026-10-13. All entries.

Seams: S2

Status

Issues #4824, #4786, #4828, #4774 and #4928 were open on 2026-10-13 (issue #4928, 1 comment, last activity 2026-10-08). The Digital Product Passport vocabulary (PR #4871, merged 2026-08-17) is the file data/ext/pending/issue-4870.ttl on main as read on 2026-10-03; the release notes of V30.1 (2026-09-16) read "Add vocabulary to support discovery of EU Digital Product Passports" and attribute to PR #4871 the class DigitalProductPassport and the property hasDigitalProductPassport for use on Product and Offer (release notes read 2026-10-14), and the term page schema.org/hasDigitalProductPassport exists (2026-10-13).

Relevance

Issue #4824 proposes a direct booking channel for LodgingBusiness; the author of the Vacation Rental Protocol commented on 2026-09-02: "For machine consumers that distinction should be verifiable, not only declared." PR #4871 links Product, and also Offer, to a passport by hasDigitalProductPassport, a sub-property of hasCertification; a reviewer wrote "Privacy will also need some consideration if "product" passports are not to become proxy identifiers for their holders, traders and purchasers." PR #4861 (2026-07-23) says "This puts several pending schema types and properties into GA (General Availability)."

Committed next

None declared in the sources read.
