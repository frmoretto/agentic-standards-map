GS1 EPCIS and Core Business Vocabulary (CBV)

GS1specificationcontext entry

Verified 2026-11-09. Status as of 2026-11-09. All entries.

Seams: S2

Status

The EPCIS archive on ref.gs1.org gives 2.0.1 as the latest version, published 2025-07-01, while the PDF served at /standards/epcis/2.0.1/ prints "Release 2.0, Ratified, Jun 2022" and does not contain the string 2.0.1. The archive pairs EPCIS 2.0.1 with the Core Business Vocabulary (CBV) 2.0.0, published 2022-06-22.

Relevance

EPCIS data consist of "visibility events", each the record of a business process step acting on objects such as trade items (products), and implementations SHALL understand queries in both EPC URN and GS1 Digital Link URI syntax (sections 1, 7). The GS1 Web Vocabulary defines the link type gs1:epcis, "A link to an EPCIS repository of visibility event data.", and the GS1-Conformant Resolver 1.2.1 names it in an example of traceability information (section 2.5.10).

Committed next

None declared in the sources read.

Boundary

The specification "does not specify how the service operations or databases themselves should be implemented" (section 1), and definitions of master data such as product catalogue data are outside its scope (section 7).
