IAB Tech Lab AAMP 3.0 and OpenProposal (agentic advertising, draft)

Industrywork-itemcontext entry

Verified 2026-10-23. Status as of 2026-10-23. All entries.

Status

IAB Tech Lab announced AAMP 3.0, "the next major release of its Agentic Advertising Management Protocols framework", on 2026-09-22, with the specification OpenProposal, whose public comment period ran "until October 22, 2026". The README of IABTechLab/OpenProposal says "Nothing here is a ratified IAB Tech Lab standard.", and the specification at tag v3.0-draft-1 (2026-09-20) is headed "AAMP 3.0 · Draft for public comment" (read 2026-10-23).

Relevance

The README of IABTechLab/AAMP says "AAMP is the umbrella initiative under which all of Tech Lab's Agentic work is being developed in partnership with the advertising ecosystem."; OpenProposal "defines how sellers can represent advertising products and respond to buyer briefs in a standardized, agent-readable format", and the release describes AAMP 3.0 as a way "for buyers and sellers to move from an RFP through proposal evaluation and toward a buy commitment". Open proposals in its repositories, all open on 2026-10-23, touch discovery and agent identity: a manifest at /.well-known/openproposal.json (OpenProposal issue #29) and IETF Web Bot Auth as the agent identity and request-signing profile (AAMP issue #3); see Open proposals.
