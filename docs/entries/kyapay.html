Current revisions, read 2026-11-09: kyapay-token-03 and using-kyapay-tokens-02, both published on the datatracker on 2026-11-02. On 2026-10-02 the author announced new revisions of five drafts: kyapay-token-02, using-kyapay-tokens-01, amr-values-02, id-verification-02 and aml-methods-01 (datatracker date 2026-10-01 for all five). The datatracker lists two more documents of the family, kyapay-token-exchange-01 (2026-07-19) and kyapayprofile-02 (2026-06-20, replaced by kyapay-token), seven in all. The Technical Plenary and Advisory Committee (TPAC) breakout proposal #39 (KYAPay: Verified Agent Identity and Payments for Ubiquitous Agentic Commerce) is open on 2026-10-13 with one comment and no assigned slot; the datatracker shows no revision of kyapay-token-exchange after -01 of 2026-07-19.

Relevance

KYAPay defines a signed JWT carrying verified agent and human identity (KYA) and payment credentials (PAY), so that merchant security infrastructure can distinguish human-authorised agents from malicious bots. The author's announcement of 2026-10-02 describes the token as a "JWT token providing claims about the person or organization authorizing an agent to act, the agent platform, the agent instance, and optionally a payment method". On the oauth list the token is discussed among bot managers, CDNs and site policy engines.
