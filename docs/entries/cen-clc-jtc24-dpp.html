CEN/CENELEC JTC 24 Digital Product Passport standards (EN 18216 to EN 18246)

Other standards bodiesspecificationcore entry

Verified 2026-10-13. Status as of 2026-10-13. All entries.

Status

Eight horizontal standards; six cited in the Official Journal of the EU according to the CEN-CENELEC page of 2026-07-15 (page re-read on 2026-10-13, unchanged); the two not cited are EN 18239 and EN 18246, and their status is not read. The Official Journal itself was not read: EUR-Lex answered HTTP 202 without content on 2026-10-13.

Relevance

The eight standards cover data exchange protocols (EN 18216), unique identifiers (EN 18219), data carriers (EN 18220), data storage (EN 18221), APIs for lifecycle management and searchability (EN 18222), system interoperability (EN 18223), access rights and security (EN 18239) and data authentication and integrity (EN 18246).

Two concern how a product is addressed and found: "unique identifiers" (EN 18219) and "searchability" (EN 18222).

Committed next

  • additional standards by product-specific Technical Committees under the 5-year ESPR work plan 2025-2030 (undated) [maintainer or chair statement] https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2026/en-in-the-spotlight/2026-07-15-dpp/ (opens in a new tab)

Boundary

Horizontal standards only; product-specific requirements are left to product-specific Technical Committees.
