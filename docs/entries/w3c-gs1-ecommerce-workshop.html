W3C/GS1 Workshop on E-commerce for Humans and AI Agents (Zurich, 8-9 Sep 2026)

W3Ceventcore entry

Verified 2026-11-02. Status as of 2026-10-13. All entries.

Seams: S2

Status

Workshop held 2026-09-08/09 at Google Zurich, co-organised by W3C and GS1. A report and follow-up session is proposed for Technical Plenary and Advisory Committee (TPAC) 2026 (breakout #34, open on 2026-10-13 with one comment and no assigned slot). A workshop report: not found in the root of the workshop repository nor in its commits (the last push is of 2026-09-09), read 2026-10-13.

Relevance

The workshop covers merchants' context, legal accountability, protocols, trust and ownership, product data, identity and authorisation, and payment; position statements (issues #10 to #34 and #45, and three PDFs in position_statement/ added by PR #43, merged 2026-07-17: Fabacus, KYA-OS, P&G) are each the statement of its author.

On seller authority and product identity, position statement #31 by Sven Böckelmann says "Resolution says where the data is; a signature says who signed it. Neither says who may speak for the product."; #21 (GS1 Digital Link) is related. On agent identity, delegation and consent, statements include #22 (FIDO Alliance Payments Technical Working Group (TWG), which says AP2 and Verifiable Intent are "now under review within the PTWG") #26 (Skyfire) and the KYA-OS position statement (PDF), which says agent authorization should be "a rail-agnostic, cross-protocol grant: bounded by scope, spend, and time, chained to an accountable party" and that "a delegation can scope authority to a schema.org Offer or a GS1 Digital Link-identified product class"; its author proposed this addition in issue #21 of this map's repository and states being the editor of the KYA-OS specification, stewarded by the DIF Trusted AI Agents Working Group, and working at Vouched, which donated KYA-OS to DIF and sells commercial products built on it.

Committed next
