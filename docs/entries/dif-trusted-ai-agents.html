DIF Trusted AI Agents Working Group

Linux Foundationvenuecontext entry

Verified 2026-10-15. Status as of 2026-10-13. All entries.

Status

Active; Delegated Authority and KYA-OS task forces; weekly meetings.

Relevance

The group defines "an opinionated, interoperable stack to enable trustworthy, privacy-preserving, and secure AI agents"; KYA-OS (donated by Vouched, March 2026) covers agent identity and delegation with DIDs and VCs.

Committed next
