Individual IETF drafts on the seller side

IETFwork-itemcore entry

Verified 2026-11-02. Status as of 2026-11-02. All entries.

Status

Three individual Internet-Drafts on the seller side of agent transactions, none adopted by a Working Group (WG): anders merchant-identity-assertions -01, kavian offering-discovery-protocol -01, hood agtp-merchant-identity (-03, dated 30 October 2026, Informational, expires 3 May 2027; -00, -02 and -03 read). The same author's draft-hood-agtp-commerce-01 (30 October 2026, Informational, read 2026-11-02) specifies a message format for agent-to-agent transactions over AGTP and says that it "moves no money, holds no funds, and operates as no payment processor".

Relevance

draft-anders-merchant-identity-assertions defines a Merchant Identity Assertion, a signed JSON document binding a legal identity to the merchant receiving a payment, so that an automated client can verify the merchant before completing it. draft-kavian-offering-discovery-protocol lets an agent discover a Service's Collections and Offerings and find links to subsequent operations, for "catalogs ranging from a few Offerings to large marketplaces without imposing a universal product taxonomy". draft-hood-agtp-merchant-identity treats merchant identity as the receiving side of a purchase (-02: "This is the merchant identity gap."); in -03 a merchant is an agent whose Agent Identity Document carries role "merchant", and the abstract says of the asymmetry between the two sides "That asymmetry is the gap this document closes."

Committed next

None declared in the sources read.

Boundary

draft-kavian-offering-discovery-protocol declines a product identifier scheme: its discovery is "without imposing a universal product taxonomy".
