IETF WIMSE, AI Identity Management System (draft-ietf-wimse-aims)

IETFwork-itemcontext entry

Verified 2026-11-09. Status as of 2026-11-09. All entries.

Status

Working Group (WG) document draft-ietf-wimse-aims-00 (2026-09-15), Informational; adopted 2026-09-09, expires 2027-03-19; replaces draft-klrc-aiagent-auth. The datatracker record of 2026-10-13 gives revision 00 (time 2026-09-15 14:40 UTC) and no later revision. On 2026-11-02 the datatracker still gives revision 00. The individual draft draft-zambo-aer1 (AER-1, revision -26 posted 2026-10-23; datatracker, read 2026-11-02) specifies "a small vocabulary for recording one AI agent tool call as a portable, independently checkable execution receipt"; it is not a WG document, and its author proposed it in issue #19 of this map's repository. Its author publishes a conformance corpus at zambo.dev/aer1/corpus.json (format aer1-evaluated-corpus/1, kit 1.5.7, referring to draft-zambo-aer1-26, 143 vectors and 146 checks, with SHA-256 digests in the index; read 2026-11-03 and again 2026-11-09, unchanged); the index shows that the corpus exists, not that the checks pass, and its scripts were not run. A companion individual draft, draft-zambo-aer1-wimse-mapping, is at revision -02 of 2026-11-01 (datatracker, read 2026-11-09; text not read). The individual draft draft-hulea-wimse-agent-mandate-00 (2026-10-24, not a WG document) cites draft-ietf-wimse-aims and defines how a relying party in one administrative domain validates the mandate under which an agent acts for a principal in another domain, adding evidence that the delegator was entitled to grant it, kept separate from the grant itself; its examples include an agent initiating a payment at a bank (text read 2026-11-02; revision -01 of 2026-11-01 is on the datatracker, read 2026-11-09, and its text was not read for this map).

Relevance

The draft "proposes best practices for authentication and authorization of AI agent interactions"; commerce, merchant, seller or purchase text: not found in -00, 2026-10-02. ACP and AP2 appear only in the references.
