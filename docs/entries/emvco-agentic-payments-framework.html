EMV Agentic Payments Framework for Specifications (draft for public feedback)

Industryspecificationcore entry

Verified 2026-10-13. Status as of 2026-10-13. All entries.

Seams: S4

Status

Draft published for feedback on 2026-09-01; the comment period ended on 2026-09-30. On 2026-10-13 the EMVCo news list has no item on the outcome of the comment period; the newsletter Q3 2026 (2026-10-09) still calls it a draft and says it was published "to provide a foundation for further industry engagement and potential specification development"; no final version or new draft is found (EMVCo news list and resource page, 2026-10-13).

Relevance

The press release of 2026-09-01 says the draft framework for card payments introduces "Intent Services", a shared layer to "register, reference, retrieve and manage consumer-authorised intent before, during and after a transaction", for cases where a consumer delegates authority to an AI agent to buy on their behalf.

It says EMVCo has established a dedicated Agentic Payments Task Force and collaborates with the FIDO Alliance, the OpenID Foundation, the OpenWallet Foundation and W3C.

Committed next

None declared in the sources read.

Boundary

EMVCo "may consider capabilities related to Know Your Agent (KYA) and Agentic Transaction Indicators to be included in future publications".
